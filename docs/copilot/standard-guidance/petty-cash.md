Staff expense reimbursements under Rs. 10,000 are processed via Petty Cash Voucher (`PV-`):

### Operational Steps:
1. Attach physical receipt or vendor invoice.
2. Obtain Department Lead sign-off.
3. Cashier disburses from Cash in Hand (`1110`).

> [!NOTE]
> Claims over Rs. 10,000 require Bank Transfer Payment Voucher.
